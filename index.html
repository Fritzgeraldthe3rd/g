Merged, not replaced

One collection, no loose ends.

This launcher sits on top of the original six game folders in Fritzgeraldthe3rd/g. Nothing was moved or overwritten: the existing Unity and HTML5 builds keep their own files, and each card opens the local copy.

Cookie Umpire brings the clicker and its desktop shell through its published build, embedded here. The arcade classics are installed locally: 2048 is MIT, and Snake, Pong and Breakout are CC0. The five third-party games remain under their own terms.

Missing entry points were added for 1v1.LOL, and local-path fixes make Bad Piggies and Baldi’s Basics run from a normal static server. The old 2048 analytics call was removed.

Before you press play

  • Use a local server; file:// will break the Unity WebGL builds.
  • The third-party games may contact their own servers for online features.
  • 2048 is MIT. Snake, Pong and Breakout are CC0; the shared license is in arcade/LICENSE-CC0.txt.
  • No license was supplied for the five original third-party games. They are not relicensed here.
  • Cookie Umpire is embedded from its published build; its source and assets are not duplicated in this repo.

Read third-party notices and the collection README.
